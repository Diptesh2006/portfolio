01 · AI reliability platform

Turbulence

LLM applications fail in more ways than a happy-path demo suggests. Turbulence makes those failures testable: it simulates outages, latency spikes, malformed responses, and routing failures so teams can compare fallback behaviour, quality, and resilience before production.

Built: reproducible evaluation scenarios, provider comparisons, and an SSE monitoring cockpit. Proof: 15 passing test fixtures.

04 · Peer-reviewed research

Accident risk prediction

Urban risk changes with place, time, traffic, weather, and the built environment. This research combines official accident records with temporal, environmental, traffic, and satellite-derived features to identify hotspots and forecast risk.

Built: the Google Earth Engine feature-extraction and spatio-temporal modelling pipeline. Outcome: peer-reviewed IEEE InGARSS publication.
